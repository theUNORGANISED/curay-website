What personal information do we process?

When you visit, use, or navigate our Services, we may process personal information depending on how you interact with us and the Services, the choices you make, and the products and features you use. This includes account information, wishlist and item data, push notification tokens, subscription and purchase data, and product URLs you submit.

Do we process any sensitive personal information?

We do not process sensitive personal information.

Do we collect any information from third parties?

We do not collect personal information from third-party social media platforms or marketing partners. We do receive subscription and purchase data from RevenueCat, our payment processing partner.

We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. We may also process your information for other purposes with your consent. We process your information only when we have a valid legal reason to do so.

In what situations and with which parties do we share personal information?

We share data with Firebase (Google) for database and storage, RevenueCat for subscription management, and Expo for push notifications. We do not sell your personal information.

What are your rights?

Depending on where you are located geographically, the applicable privacy law may mean you have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data.

How do you exercise your rights?

The easiest way to exercise your rights is by contacting us directly at curay.app@gmail.com. We will consider and act upon any request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services or otherwise interact with us. This includes:

  • Account information: your first name, last name, and email address, provided during registration.
  • Wishlist and item data: the lists you create, the items you add (including product names, prices, notes, and images), and any actions you take on those items.
  • Product URLs: when you add an item via a URL, that URL is processed by our servers to extract product metadata such as name, price, images, and store name.
  • Imported data: if you use the CSV import feature, the contents of that file are processed to create items in your account.

Sensitive information. We do not process sensitive information.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

Information automatically collected

We automatically collect certain technical information when you use the Services. This may include your device type, operating system, language preferences, device identifiers, and information about how and when you use our Services. This information is primarily needed to maintain the security and operation of our Services.

We do not use cookies or web tracking technologies in our mobile application. We do not collect GPS or precise location data.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Account management: to create and manage your user account, authenticate you, and allow you to update your profile.
  • Core app functionality: to store your wishlists and items, enable list sharing, and allow you to track prices and product availability.
  • Price and availability monitoring: we automatically monitor submitted product URLs for price changes, stock status changes, and promotional codes in order to send you activity alerts.
  • Push notifications: to send you alerts about price drops, restocks, and other activity updates you have opted into.
  • Subscription management: to manage your subscription plan, enforce feature limits, and process upgrades or downgrades via RevenueCat.
  • Import and export: to process CSV files you upload and to generate CSV exports of your data on request.
  • Security and fraud prevention: to protect our Services and our users.
  • Legal compliance: to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following situations:

  • Service providers: we share data with third-party service providers who help us operate the app, including Firebase (Google) for database, storage, and authentication, RevenueCat for subscription management, and Expo for push notification delivery. These providers are contractually required to protect your data.
  • Public lists: if you enable public sharing for a list, that list and its contents become accessible to anyone with the share link, including users who are not logged in.
  • Business transfers: we may share or transfer your information in connection with a merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business.
  • Legal obligations: we may disclose your information where required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests.

4. What Third-Party Services Do We Use?

The following third-party services process data on our behalf:

  • Firebase (Google): we use Firebase for user authentication, database storage (Firestore), file storage (Cloud Storage), and server-side functions. Your account data, wishlist data, and uploaded images are stored in Firebase. Firebase servers relevant to our app are located in Germany. See Firebase's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: we use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat receives your subscription status, purchase history, and device identifiers necessary to validate and manage your plan. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo (push notifications): we use Expo's push notification service to deliver alerts to your device. To do this, a push notification token is generated for your device and stored securely. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising networks, analytics platforms, session recording tools, or social login providers.

5. How Do We Handle Push Notifications?

With your permission, we collect a push notification token for your device. This token is used solely to deliver activity alerts you have opted into, such as price drop alerts, restock notifications, and promo code alerts.

You can enable or disable push notifications at any time in the app's notification settings, or through your device's system settings. Disabling notifications does not affect your core use of the app.

6. How Do We Process Product URLs?

When you add an item to your wishlist by pasting a product URL, that URL is sent to our servers, where automated tools extract product information such as the item name, price, images, and store name. This processing happens server-side and the extracted data is stored in your account.

We also periodically re-process stored product URLs to monitor for price changes, stock status updates, and promotional codes, in order to generate activity alerts for you.

We do not use product URLs for any purpose other than providing this functionality to you.

7. How Do We Handle Subscriptions and Payments?

Curay offers free and paid subscription plans. Subscription purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat as our subscription management platform to track your plan status, validate entitlements, and handle upgrades and downgrades.

We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple and RevenueCat in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

8. Is Your Information Transferred Internationally?

Our primary Firebase servers are located in Germany. However, some of our third-party service providers, including RevenueCat and Expo, may process data in the United States or other countries.

If you are a resident in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), or Switzerland, please be aware that some countries may not have data protection laws as comprehensive as those in your region. We take appropriate measures to ensure your data is protected in accordance with this Privacy Notice and applicable law, including relying on standard contractual clauses where required.

9. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as tax, accounting, or other legal requirements).

When you delete your account, we will delete your personal information from our active databases. Some information may be retained in backup archives for a limited period before being permanently deleted. When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will either delete or anonymize it.

10. Do We Collect Information From Minors?

We do not knowingly collect, solicit data from, or market to children under 18 years of age, nor do we knowingly sell such personal information. By using the Services, you represent that you are at least 18, or that you are the parent or guardian of such a minor and consent to such minor dependent's use of the Services.

If we learn that personal information from users less than 18 years of age has been collected, we will deactivate the account and take reasonable measures to promptly delete such data from our records. If you become aware of any data we may have collected from children under age 18, please contact us at curay.app@gmail.com.

You may review, change, or terminate your account at any time. Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete information

  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • The right to data portability (you can export your data as a CSV from within the app)
  • The right to object to or restrict certain types of processing

Withdrawing your consent

If we are relying on your consent to process your personal information, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. This will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before your withdrawal.

Account information

You can review and update your account information (name and email) directly within the app. You can also delete your account from within the app's settings. Upon account deletion, we will remove your personal data from our active databases.
